The repository identifies its 3D scene as original work. Scrollfly reuses the
fly geometry from dist/scene.js and foreleg kinematics from dist/swipe.js.
The repository's THIRD_PARTY.md identifies separate licenses for its neural
backend, data and Three.js; it does not specify a general scene-code license.
The scene source is credited separately from the MIT-licensed Three.js runtime.

Changes: extracted the fly from the chamber into a reusable component, moved
its root to the existing scene, and adapted its idle, celebration and gesture
clocks. Its geometry, materials, six legs, wings, head implant and fixed-length
foreleg kinematics come from the pinned source. The chamber, tether, garden,
phone, neural backend and videos are not imported. The implant is decorative.
The original Three.js dependency is the same r180 version already vendored.
